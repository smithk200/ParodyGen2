How to get the Magic Muffler

1. After defeating the Kanto storyline, challenge the Elite Four in Kanto 10 times. 
Note: if you use an item, there is a 20% chance that it may be more than 10, as each time you use an item, the tracker will have a 20% chance to remove a victory.
2. After defeating the Elite Four in Kanto 10 times, challenge 100 Kanto trainers. The Vs. Seeker is allowed. Note: this only works in the Kanto region.
3. After defeating 100 Kanto trainers, catch a Dratini. You can catch one in the Safari Zone in Fuchsia City.
4. Once you catch a Dratini, you should see a hiker in Fuchsia City. He'll give you a Salac Berry if you have a Dragonite in your party.
5. Train Dratini until it evolves into Dragonite. Then you can get the Salac Berry from the hiker.
6. Make Dragonite hold the Salac Berry.
7. Challenge the Elite Four 5 times. Note that you must win the fights!
8. Go to Prof. Elm in New Bark Town... then use Fly 3 times.
9. Get Dragonite in your party and take the Salac Berry from it. The Salac Berry it holds will be a Magic Muffler.

Q. What does the Magic Muffler do?
A. It makes your Pokémon's experience move fast. If you have a level 5 Squirtle and it's holding the Magic Muffler...
it will be at level 97 after just one fight.

This is 100% real and mostly bugtested!
